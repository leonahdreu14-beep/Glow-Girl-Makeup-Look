Douyin Makeup: Step-by-Step Guide

- Prep your skin: Cleanse, moisturize, and apply sunscreen. Use a primer if you want makeup to last longer.

- Even out your complexion: Apply a thin layer of foundation or cushion, then conceal blemishes and under-eye shadows. Set the T-zone lightly with powder.

- Shape your brows: Fill them softly with short strokes and brush the hairs into place.

- Create soft eye definition: Apply a light neutral shadow over the lid, a deeper shade at the outer corner, and a subtle shimmer at the center or inner corner.

- Draw eyeliner: Make a thin line along the upper lashes, extending it slightly outward for a gentle lifted effect.

- Add lashes: Curl your lashes and apply mascara, or use natural-looking false lashes.

- Add blush: Sweep a rosy or peach blush across the cheeks and blend toward the temples.

- Highlight: Apply a small amount to the inner corners of the eyes, cheekbones, and nose bridge.

- Finish the lips: Use a lip tint or lipstick, concentrating color near the center and softly blending outward.

- Set your makeup: Use setting spray, then check the look in natural light.
