Igary Makeup

1. Prep your skin with moisturizer and sunscreen.

2. Apply a light skin tint and blend.

3. Dab rosy blush across your cheeks and nose.

4. Brush your brows into a natural shape.

5. Add neutral eyeshadow, thin brown liner, and mascara.

6. Finish with tinted lip balm and setting spray.

Soft, rosy, and ready to go!
