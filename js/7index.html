Lip combo



- Glossy Lips: Add lip gloss for a shiny, fresh finish.

- Soft Matte Lips: Apply matte lipstick for a smooth, velvety look.

- Ombre Lips: Use a deeper shade in the center and blend it outward.

- Blurred Lips: Tap lip tint onto your lips and soften the edges.

- Bold Lips: Choose a vibrant shade for a statement look.
