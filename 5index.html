Baddie Makeup

1.Prep your skin: Cleanse and moisturize your face, then apply primer to create a smooth base.

2.Apply foundation: Blend foundation evenly over your face and down your neck for a seamless finish.

3.Brighten with concealer: Add concealer under your eyes and to areas you want to highlight, then blend it out.

4.Add definition: Apply contour beneath your cheekbones, along your jawline, and around your nose. Blend well to avoid harsh lines.

5.Bring warmth and glow: Sweep blush onto your cheeks and add highlighter to your cheekbones and other high points.

6.Shape your brows: Fill in sparse areas with a brow pencil or powder, then brush the hairs into place.

7.Create a bold eye look: Blend a neutral shade over your lids, then add a deeper color to the crease and outer corners. Finish with eyeliner and mascara or lashes.

8.Finish your lips: Outline your lips with liner, then apply lipstick or gloss for a fuller-looking finish.

9.Set your makeup: Lightly apply setting powder where needed, then use setting spray to help your look last.
